# Solo Missions

**10 hard things. No gaps. No excuses.**

🌐 **Live → https://mavinash-dev.github.io/solo-missions/**

---

A single-file web app to track 10 discipline challenges. No backend, no dependencies — everything lives in your browser's `localStorage`.

## Missions

| # | Mission | Type |
|---|---------|------|
| 1 | Walk 30–50KM Alone | One shot |
| 2 | 21 Days — Zero Sugar | 21 days |
| 3 | Train in Silence — 7 Days | 7 days |
| 4 | Wake Up at 5AM — 30 Days | 30 days |
| 5 | 7 Days — No Social Media | 7 days |
| 6 | Cold Shower — 30 Days | 30 days |
| 7 | 24 Hours — No Phone | One shot |
| 8 | Read 1 Book in 1 Day | One shot |
| 9 | Go Out Alone — No Phone | One shot |
| 10 | Sleep Before 10PM — 30 Days | 30 days |

## Rules

- **No gaps allowed** on daily missions — miss a day and you reset. That's the rule.
- One-shot missions are marked done once. No undoing.
- Progress is stored locally — no account, no server.

## Dev preview

Add `?win=<id>` to the URL to preview any completion animation:

```
?win=sugar   ?win=shower   ?win=walk   ?win=sleep
```
